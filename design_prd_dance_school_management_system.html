# Project: Multi-Branch Dance School Management System ## Visual Identity & Brand Direction A "Cultural Fusion" aesthetic that blends the traditional richness of Indian classical dance (Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Odissi) with the clean, functional efficiency of a modern SaaS dashboard. ### Core Principles - **Elegance & Artistry:** Use motifs and silhouettes to evoke the spirit of dance. - **Premium Professionalism:** Maintain high usability, clear hierarchy, and modern spacing. - **Clarity:** Decorative elements should enhance the experience, not clutter it. ## Design Tokens - **Primary:** Deep Maroon / Burgundy (#800000) - **Secondary:** Gold / Muted Saffron (#D4AF37 / #F4C430) - **Background:** Off-white / Ivory / Light Beige (#FCF9F2) - **Accent:** Dark Brown / Charcoal (#332211) - **Typography:** - Headings: Serif (e.g., Playfair Display) for a royal, traditional feel. - Body: Sans-serif (e.g., Poppins/Inter) for readability. ## Component Library (Planned) - **Cards:** Rounded corners, soft shadows, subtle gold borders. - **Buttons:** Primary (Maroon with Gold text/accents), Secondary (Ghost with Gold border). - **Navigation:** Sidebar with subtle Indian motifs in the background; Sticky Topbar. - **Tables:** Clean, high-contrast text with delicate row separators. - **Dividers:** Rangoli or temple-border inspired ornamental lines. ## User Flows & Screens 1. **Public Landing:** Hero section, Style Showcase, Branch Cards, Testimonials. 2. **Auth:** Centered, minimal cards with soft gold accents. 3. **Admin Dashboard:** High-level stats, enrollment charts, and branch overview. 4. **Course Catalog:** Grid of styles with price/duration/branch info. 5. **Enrollment Flow:** Batch details followed by a graceful Admission Form.
